Short Story · Fiction

The Last Bus Home

Sample piece (made up, to show how it works)

A night-shift nurse, a stranded music student, and one snowy ride across town that changes how they both see the city.

The 11:40 was always late on snow nights, and Nadia had stopped pretending to mind. She liked the hush of the shelter, the way the streetlights turned every flake into something worth noticing.

The boy with the cello case arrived at 11:52, breathless, sure he'd missed it. "You didn't," she told him, before he could ask. "Nobody ever really misses the last one. It waits for us."

It's a small thing, a bus that runs late. But that night, it was the reason two strangers ended up talking about everything they'd been too tired to say out loud all year.
