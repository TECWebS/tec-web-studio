General Web Services Agreement

The terms for TEC Web Studio website packages and specials.

TEC Web Studio LLC · Version 1.1 · Effective October 22, 2026

This is the agreement you read and sign online before paying for a website package or special. Your signed copy also includes your Order Summary, your initials, and both signatures, and we email it to you.

1. PARTIES

This General Web Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between TEC Web Studio LLC, a Connecticut limited liability company with its principal place of business at 24 Brewer Street, New London, CT 06320 (“Studio”), and the person or business entity identified at the time of engagement (“Client”).

The Studio’s Managing Member is authorized to execute this Agreement on behalf of TEC Web Studio LLC pursuant to the Connecticut Limited Liability Company Act.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Services. Studio will provide web design and related services as described in the project proposal or order form accepted by Client, including the Order Summary presented to Client before signing (“Project”). The Project price, any selected add-ons, and the total due are as shown in the Order Summary.

2.2 No Guarantee of Results. Studio makes no representation that any website will generate specific traffic, leads, revenue, or search engine rankings. Deliverables are limited to the functional and design outputs described in the Project proposal.

2.3 Client Responsibilities. Client shall provide all content, materials, credentials, and approvals necessary for Studio to complete the Project within agreed timelines. Delays caused by Client, including late content, materials, or approvals, extend the Project timeline accordingly, and Studio is not responsible for those delays.

2.4 Delivery Timeframe. Studio will deliver the finished website within five (5) to ten (10) business days after the later of (a) Studio’s receipt of payment and (b) Studio’s receipt of all content and materials needed for the Project (“Standard Delivery”). If Client selects priority delivery, the delivery deadline shown in the Order Summary applies instead. “Business days” means Monday through Friday, excluding federal and Connecticut state holidays. The website is “delivered” when Studio sends Client a link to the finished website for review.

2.5 Orders by Adults Only. Client must be at least eighteen (18) years old to place an order and accept this Agreement. A website for a person under eighteen (18), such as a student portfolio, must be ordered by that person’s parent or legal guardian, who is the Client under this Agreement.

2.6 Content About Other People. Client is responsible for the names, photographs, and other information about other people that Client provides for the website. Client confirms that Client has permission from each person named or pictured, and, for any person under eighteen (18), from that person’s parent or legal guardian, to publish that information on the website. Studio may decline to publish, or may remove, any information about a minor if Studio reasonably believes permission has not been given or the information could put the minor at risk.

3. FREE AUDIT PROVISION

Studio may offer a complimentary website audit prior to engagement. No payment is required for the audit. The audit does not create a binding obligation on either party. If Client approves a Project following the audit, payment in full is due before Studio begins any work.

4. PAYMENT TERMS

4.1 Payment in Full. All Project fees are due in full before Studio begins work. Studio will not commence any Project until payment is received and confirmed.

4.2 No Payment Invoices; Receipts Only. Studio does not issue invoices requesting payment. All fees are paid at the time of Project approval through Studio’s designated payment method. Studio provides Client a receipt for every payment, including any recurring plan charges.

4.3 Pre-Commitment Disclosure. The total Project fee, including any selected add-ons, any discount, and any applicable sales tax, will be clearly disclosed to Client in the Order Summary before Client signs this Agreement and before payment is collected.

4.4 Sales Tax. Studio charges Connecticut sales tax as required by Connecticut law. Clients located in Connecticut pay sales tax at the rate that applies to the use of the services (currently 1% for business use and 6.35% for personal use). Clients located outside Connecticut are not charged Connecticut sales tax. Any discount is applied before sales tax is calculated. A Client who orders for business use certifies that the website is for business use and that the business information Client provides is accurate. If that certification is inaccurate, Client is responsible for any additional sales tax, interest, and penalties that result.

5. REVISIONS AND CHANGE ORDERS

5.1 Included Revisions. Each Project includes the number of revision rounds shown in the Order Summary.

5.2 Out-of-Scope Work. Any work requested beyond the original Project scope or beyond the included revision rounds constitutes a change order. Studio will provide a written change order specifying the additional work and fee. Work on the change order begins only after Client signs the change order and pays any required fee.

5.3 Scope Creep. Requests that materially alter the Project’s design, structure, or functionality are out-of-scope regardless of whether revision rounds remain.

6.2 Studio-Retained Assets. Studio retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, code libraries, workflows, and any other reusable materials developed independently of Client’s Project (“Studio IP”), including those incorporated into Client’s deliverable. 6.3 No Assignment of Studio IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Studio IP to Client.

10.4 Exceptions. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known to the Receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party’s information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party before disclosure.

11. TERMINATION

11.1 Termination by Either Party. Either party may terminate this Agreement with written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days of written notice.

11.2 Cancellation by Client and Refunds. Client may cancel the Project by written notice to Studio (email is sufficient). Refunds are based on when Studio receives the notice, counted in calendar days from the date of payment:

  • Days 1 through 3: a full refund of all fees paid, except any priority delivery fee.

  • Days 4 through 7: a refund of fifty percent (50%) of all fees paid, except any priority delivery fee.

  • After Day 7: no refund.

A priority delivery fee is not refundable upon cancellation, except as provided in Section 11.5. Sales tax is refunded in the same proportion as the fees refunded. Refunds are made to the original payment method.

11.3 Termination by Studio. Studio may terminate this Agreement if Client fails to provide necessary materials or approvals for more than thirty (30) days, or if Client engages in conduct that Studio reasonably determines makes continued performance impractical. In such case, Studio will refund a pro-rated portion of fees corresponding to work not yet completed, less any third-party costs incurred.

11.4 Survival. Sections 2.6, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15, and 16 survive termination.

11.5 Late Delivery Credit. If Studio does not deliver the website by the end of the Standard Delivery period or by the priority delivery deadline shown in the Order Summary, Studio will refund five percent (5%) of the Project fee for each business day the delivery is late, up to a maximum of fifty percent (50%). If Client paid for priority delivery and Studio misses the priority deadline, Studio will also refund the priority delivery fee in full. If delivery is more than ten (10) business days late, Client may instead cancel by written notice and receive a full refund of all amounts paid, less any late-delivery credits already refunded. Days of delay caused by Client (Section 2.3) or by events described in Section 13 do not count as late. Sales tax is refunded in the same proportion as the fees refunded. For this Section, “Project fee” means the package price after any discount, not including priority delivery fees or sales tax.

12.2 Mediation. If negotiation fails, the parties shall submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) or a mutually agreed mediator in New London County, Connecticut. The parties share the mediator’s fees equally.

12.3 Binding Arbitration. If the dispute is not resolved within sixty (60) days after mediation begins, either party may submit it to final and binding arbitration administered by the AAA before a single arbitrator. The arbitration will be conducted under the AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules, or, if Client is an individual using the services for personal or household purposes, the AAA Consumer Arbitration Rules. The arbitration will take place in New London County, Connecticut, or by video conference if both parties agree. The arbitrator may award the same relief a court could award on an individual claim, and the arbitrator’s decision is final. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court with jurisdiction.

12.4 Governing Arbitration Law. This Section 12 is governed by the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. § 1 et seq., and, to the extent not inconsistent with it, Conn. Gen. Stat. § 52-408 et seq.

12.5 Exceptions. 12.6 Fees. Arbitration filing, administrative, and arbitrator fees are paid as provided in the applicable AAA rules. Each party pays its own attorney’s fees, unless the law or the arbitrator awards them.

12.7 Individual Claims Only. Claims may be brought only on an individual basis, not as a plaintiff or class member in any class, collective, or representative proceeding.

12.8 Waiver of Jury Trial. BY ACCEPTING THIS AGREEMENT, CLIENT AND STUDIO EACH GIVE UP THE RIGHT TO HAVE A DISPUTE DECIDED BY A JUDGE OR JURY, EXCEPT AS STATED IN SECTION 12.5.
