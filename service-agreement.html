Website Services Agreement

Plain-language terms for TEC Web Studio website packages and specials.

Version 1.0 draft, October 14, 2026 · Applies to Quick Start, Business Website, Complete Brand + Website, and the Mother’s Day Memory Page, Family Tree Build, and Student Portfolio specials. (Site Starter has its own agreement.)

For the reviewer: how clients see and sign this

  • A client sends a request (nothing is charged). TEC Web Studio reviews it and emails a private link. For specials, the link is sent after the client approves a sample.
  • The page shows the Order Summary below, filled in for that client, followed by the agreement text.
  • For website packages, the page first links to the client’s preview site. When signing, the client chooses “I approve the preview” or “I approve the preview, with these changes” and types one list of changes. That choice and list are added to the Order Summary of the signed copy.
  • The client checks “I have read and agree” and types their full name, then selects “Sign agreement.” Only then does the payment form appear. Payment is in full.
  • On signing, the system saves the typed name, email, date and time, IP address, browser, the agreement version, and the exact text shown, with its SHA-256 fingerprint. The signed copy is emailed to the client and to TEC Web Studio.

The short version

  • What you get: the package, special, and add-ons in your Order Summary, nothing more and nothing less. Extra work is quoted separately in writing.
  • Payment: you pay in full after you sign. Work starts once you have paid and we have your content.
  • Your part: you send your text, logo, photos, and details on time, and you have the right to use everything you send.
  • Your preview: you approve your preview when you sign, either as it is or with one list of changes we will include.
  • Revisions: the number of revision rounds in your package, on the finished website. More changes can be added for a fee.
  • Ownership: once you have paid in full, the finished website we design for you is yours. Your domain is always yours.
  • Some sites aren’t allowed: we don’t build or host anything that spreads hate, promotes violence, deceives people, or breaks the law (see Section 6).
  • Refunds: depend on how far your project has gone (see Section 10).

1. Your project

We will design and build the website described in your Order Summary. What each package and special includes is the description on tecwebstudio.com on the date you sign, which we summarize here:

  • Quick Start: a custom-designed, mobile-friendly website of 1–4 pages, a contact form, social media links, basic SEO setup, 1 revision round, and 1 month of maintenance.
  • Business Website: a custom-designed website of up to 6 pages, plus a contact or quote form, click-to-call, Google Maps, a services section, reviews or testimonials, basic animations, basic SEO setup, 2 revision rounds, and 1 month of maintenance.
  • Complete Brand + Website: A custom-designed website of up to 8 pages with everything in Business Website, plus business name assistance, a custom logo, brand direction, professional website copy, service descriptions, calls-to-action, SEO setup and local business optimization, 3 revision rounds, and 2 months of maintenance with minor updates.
  • Mother’s Day Memory Page, Family Tree Build, and Student Portfolio: the special as described on its page and in the sample we showed you before you received this agreement. The Student Portfolio includes up to 4 pages and 2 revision rounds.
  • Add-ons Optional services and upgrades, including Priority Delivery, Editor Lite, Editor Plus, Website Update Pack, Website Care, and similar services, are included only if they are listed in your Order Summary. Each add-on is subject to its stated features, limits, duration, and any additional terms provided at purchase.

Anything not listed is outside this agreement. That includes online stores, booking systems, member areas, extra pages, and new features. We are happy to quote extra work, and it becomes part of your project only when we both agree to it in writing.

They renew each month until you cancel, which you can do at any time from the billing link we send you. Cancelling stops future charges at the end of the current month. The Website Update Pack is a one-time charge included in your total.

  • Your preview. For website packages, we create a preview based on the information you provide, including your preferred colors, style, and content. You can view your preview using the private link in your Order Summary.
  • Approving it. When you sign this agreement, you must choose one of the following options: Approve the preview as it is. Approve the preview and provide one combined list of changes you would like us to make before building your full website. If you request changes, we will include the agreed changes when building your website. Your approval and any requested changes become part of this agreement.
  • One Preview, Not Multiple Rounds Your package includes one preview approval stage. We build your full website based on the approved preview and any changes included in your approval. A second preview or another round of changes to the preview is not included. Requests that add pages, sections, features, or other work outside your package require a separate quote under Section 1.
  • Revisions to Your Finished Website Your package includes a set number of revision rounds, as listed in Section 1. A revision round means one combined list of changes to the finished website we present to you. Please send all changes for each round together rather than sending them separately over multiple messages. Revisions allow you to adjust the work included in your original package. They do not include new pages, sections, features, or work outside the original scope.
  • Additional ChangesAdditional revision rounds and changes requested after you approve the final website are not included in your package. Additional work will be billed at $75 per hour or quoted at a flat rate before work begins. We will explain the expected cost and obtain your approval before starting any additional work. New pages, sections, features, or other work outside your original package may require a separate quote, even if you have revision rounds remaining.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Content and photos. You will send your text, logo, photos, services, prices, hours, and other details in a usable form and on time. If your package includes copywriting or brand work, we write it and you check it for accuracy before it goes live.
  • Rights. You promise you own or have permission to use everything you send us, including photos, logos, music, and quotes, and that it does not infringe anyone else’s rights.
  • Accuracy. You are responsible for making sure what your site says about your business, products, prices, and licenses is true and legal.
  • Real reviews only. Only publish testimonials from real customers who agreed to be quoted. Fake or invented reviews are not allowed and may be illegal.
  • Stock photos. If we use licensed stock photos on your site, you may use them only on that site, unless the license allows more.
  • Feedback and access. You will review our work and respond within a reasonable time, name one person who can approve decisions, and give us any account access we need, such as access to your domain or Wix account.

6. What we won’t build or host

We’re proud to help small businesses, and we want every site we build to be one we’re glad to stand behind. For that reason, there are certain kinds of websites we will not build or host, no matter who asks.

We will not design, build, publish, or host any site (including text, photos, links, or social-media pages it points to) that:

  • Promotes hate or violence: content that threatens, encourages, or celebrates violence, or that attacks or demeans people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic. This also includes content that supports groups organized around hatred or violence.
  • Harasses or endangers people: threats, stalking, bullying, or sharing someone’s private information without permission.
  • Involves illegal activity: selling illegal goods or services, or anything that helps someone break the law.
  • Deceives or defrauds people: scams, fake reviews or credentials, false claims about products, health, or money, impersonating another person or business, phishing, or malware.
  • Harms children or exploits people: zero tolerance for any sexual content involving minors or for human trafficking. We also do not build or host sexually explicit material.
  • Preys on vulnerable people: misleading offers aimed at people in financial, medical, or emotional crisis.
  • Copies someone else’s work or otherwise infringes another person’s or business’s rights.
  • Is otherwise harmful or unethical. This list gives examples and cannot cover everything. If something is not listed but we reasonably conclude it would cause serious harm or is something we cannot put our name behind, we may decline it.

We judge a site by what it says and does, never by who the client is, and we apply these rules the same way to everyone. When we decline or remove something, we will tell you why in writing. If something comes up during your project, we will pause and give you a chance to change it first. A live site that is illegal or puts someone at risk may be taken offline immediately. For smaller issues we will give you three days’ written notice to fix the problem first. Refunds in these cases follow Section 10.
