Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take?

Most projects can be completed within 7–14 days, depending on the size of the project, revisions, and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Do I need to provide the website content?

You can provide your own content, or choose our Complete Brand + Website package and we'll help create the content for you.

Do you work with businesses outside my area?

Yes. TEC Web Studio works with businesses remotely, so you don't have to be local to work with us.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Our Website Makeover service is designed for businesses that already have a website but want to improve its appearance, usability, and overall experience.

Do you maintain websites after they're launched?

Yes. Maintenance is included for a limited period with our packages, and ongoing maintenance can be discussed after your included period.

What if I don't know what kind of website I need?

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. We'll look at your business and help determine which option makes sense for you.
