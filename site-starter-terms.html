Site Starter Agreement

Plain-language terms for the TEC Web Studio Site Starter service.

Effective [date] · Version 0.1 · Provided by TEC Web Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”)

The short version

  • What you get: a simple one-page website you set up yourself in four guided steps, hosted at your own web address, and editable any time during your term.
  • What you don’t get: custom design work, forms, videos, extra pages, or special requests. This is a fixed, simple product. If you want more, you can order a full website from us.
  • Your web address: we register it and hold it for you. We will never sell it or use it for anything else, and we will release it to you when you ask in writing (fee below).
  • Release fee: $15 whenever you ask us to transfer your domain to you.
  • Renewal: we will remind you before your term ends. Nothing renews automatically.
  • Real content only: photos and testimonials you publish must be yours and must be real.
  • Some sites aren’t allowed: we don’t host anything that spreads hate, promotes violence, deceives people, or breaks the law (see Section 4).

1. What the service includes

During your paid term you receive:

  • A guided four-step setup: choose your sections, choose a color palette and font pairing from our list, add your content, then publish.
  • One single-page website that always includes a top banner and a contact section. You may also add any of these optional sections: About, Services, Testimonials, and Gallery.
  • Limits: one top-banner photo, up to 3 services, up to 2 testimonials, and up to 3 gallery photos.
  • Hosting of your site and one web address (domain) registered for the length of your term.
  • A private login so you can view and edit only your own site as often as you like until your term ends.
  • Email support for using the service (see Section 9).

2. What the service does not include

This is a deliberately simple product. It does not include, and we do not take requests for:

  • Contact forms, booking or scheduling tools, online payments or a shop, blogs, or membership areas
  • Videos, animations, or sound
  • Additional pages, extra sections, or changes to the layout, colors, or fonts beyond the options we offer
  • Custom code, third-party add-ons, or integrations
  • Business email accounts, logo or brand design, copywriting, photography, or search-engine optimization services

We cannot add features to individual sites, even for a fee. If you outgrow this service, you can order a full custom website from TEC Web Studio at tecwebstudio.com/services.

3. Your account and your content

  • You own your content (your text, photos, and logo). You give us permission to store, display, and publish it as part of your site.
  • You promise you have the right to use everything you upload and that what you say about your business is accurate.
  • Testimonials must be real. Only publish quotes from real customers who agreed to be quoted. Fake or invented reviews are not allowed and may be illegal.
  • Follow the rules in Section 4. There are some kinds of websites we do not host, and they are spelled out there.
  • We review before anything goes live. Every site, and every update you submit, is reviewed by us before it is published (see Section 4).
  • Keep your login private. You are responsible for all activity on your account.
  • Our platform stays ours. The software, templates, and designs remain the property of TEC Web Studio. You receive a license to use the site we generate for you while your service is active.

4. What we won’t host

We’re proud to help small businesses, and we want every site we put online to be one we’re glad to stand behind. For that reason, there are certain kinds of websites we will not host, no matter who asks.

What isn’t allowed

We will not publish, host, or keep online any site (including text, photos, links, or social-media pages it points to) that:

  • Promotes hate or violence — content that threatens, encourages, or celebrates violence, or that attacks or demeans people because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic. This also includes content that supports groups organized around hatred or violence.
  • Harasses or endangers people — threats, stalking, bullying, or sharing someone’s private information without permission.
  • Involves illegal activity — selling illegal goods or services, or anything that helps someone break the law.
  • Deceives or defrauds people — scams, fake reviews or credentials, false claims about products, health, or money, impersonating another person or business, phishing, or malware.
  • Harms children or exploits people — zero tolerance for any sexual content involving minors or for human trafficking. We also do not host sexually explicit material.
  • Preys on vulnerable people — misleading offers aimed at people in financial, medical, or emotional crisis.
  • Copies someone else’s work or otherwise infringes another person’s or business’s rights.
  • Is otherwise harmful or unethical. This list gives examples and cannot cover everything. If something is not listed but we reasonably conclude it would cause serious harm or is something we cannot put our name behind, we may decline it.

How we decide

  • We judge a site by what it says and does, never by who the customer is. We are glad to work with customers of every background.
  • We apply these rules the same way to everyone. When we decline or remove something, we will tell you why in writing.
  • If we decline a site before it goes live, you are welcome to revise it and send it again.

If there’s a problem

  • A live site that breaks these rules may be taken offline immediately, without notice, if it is illegal or puts someone at risk. For smaller issues we will give you three days’ written notice to fix the problem first.
  • If we end your service because of a violation, the unused portion of your term is not refunded.
  • If your service ends because of a violation, we will still release your domain to you on written request and payment of the release fee, within the time stated in Section 6. Your site will not stay online with us, and we may point your domain to a plain “unavailable” page while we review. We may decline or delay the release only if (a) a court, law-enforcement agency, or registrar requires us to hold the domain; (b) the domain name itself breaks Section 4 or infringes someone else’s rights; (c) the domain was used for fraud, phishing, or malware and an investigation is still open; or (d) you owe us money under this agreement. If we decline or delay, we will tell you why in writing. Once the domain is released, we are not responsible for how it is used.

Legal requests

We may share information with law enforcement or others when the law requires it, or when needed to protect people from serious harm.

See something wrong?

Tell us at info@tecwebstudio.com and we will look into it.

5. Price, term, and renewal

  • Plans: 1 year for $50, 2 years for $80, or 3 years for $110. Your term starts when you pay.
  • Renewal is $30 per additional year after any plan and includes your web address, hosting, and editing access.
  • No automatic renewal. We will email you about 60, 30, and 7 days before your term ends. If you want to continue, you renew yourself.
  • If your term ends: your site stays online for a 30-day grace period, then goes offline. We keep your content for 90 days after your term ends so you can renew or export it; after that we may delete it.
  • All prices are in U.S. dollars and may change for future purchases or renewals. We will tell you before a renewal price changes.

6. Your web address (domain)

This section is important — please read it carefully.

  • We register and hold the domain. The domain is registered under TEC Web Studio’s account and name. We hold it for your benefit. We will not sell it, lend it, or point it at anything other than your site during your term.
  • You can have it. At any time you may ask us in writing to release the domain to you. We will also send you the DNS records that make your site work.
  • Release fee. A flat fee of $15 applies whenever you ask us to release the domain, whether during or after your term. This covers the registration cost and the work of the transfer. If you bought a multi-year plan and ask for the domain early, the fee remains the same and unused service time is not refunded.
  • How fast. Within 10 business days after we receive your written request and the release fee, we will unlock the domain and give you the authorization code you need to move it to a registrar of your choice.
  • After your term ends you may still request the domain for 90 days. After that we may let it expire.
  • Limits you should know about. Registrars generally cannot transfer a domain within 60 days of its initial registration. Your new registrar may charge its own fees, and you are responsible for opening an account with them. Moving the domain does not move your site: after a transfer you will need a new host, and your site will stop working here once your term ends.
  • Name rights. We register only names you tell us you have the right to use. You promise your chosen name does not infringe anyone’s trademark or other rights. We cannot guarantee any name will be available.
  • Leaving with your content. On request we will give you an export of your text and photos in the form of an HTML-coded file.

You may cancel and receive a full refund within 7 days of purchase. If we have already registered your domain, we retain the domain registration cost (approximately $12). After 7 days, payments are not refundable and unused time on your term is not credited.

If we decline to publish your site under Section 4, you may revise it and resubmit, or cancel for a full refund minus the cost of the domain if we have already registered it.
