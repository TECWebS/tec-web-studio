Site Starter Program Agreement

The terms for the Site Starter program.

TEC Web Studio LLC · Version 1.1 · Effective October 22, 2026

This is the agreement you accept at checkout when you enroll in Site Starter. We email you a copy with your initials and signature.

1. PROGRAM DESCRIPTION AND DELIVERABLES

1.1 What Site Starter Is. The Site Starter program (“Program”) is a fixed-term website hosting and setup service offered by TEC Web Studio LLC, a Connecticut limited liability company at 24 Brewer Street, New London, CT 06320 (“Studio”), to the person or business that enrolls (“Client”). Under the Program, Studio will build and host a website for Client for the duration of the selected term.

1.2 What Is Included. The Program includes website design and setup, hosting on Studio’s infrastructure, and access to Client’s site management tools for the selected term. Specific inclusions are described in the Program enrollment materials presented to Client before purchase.

1.3 What Is Not Included. The Program does not include custom development beyond the standard Site Starter template, SEO campaigns, paid advertising, or any service not expressly listed in the enrollment materials.

1.4 No Results Guarantee. Studio makes no guarantee of any specific business result, including traffic, leads, sales, or revenue, from Client’s participation in the Program.

1.5 Enrollment by Adults Only. Client must be at least eighteen (18) years old to enroll. A site for a person under eighteen (18) must be enrolled by that person’s parent or legal guardian, who is the Client under this Agreement.

1.6 Content About Other People. Client is responsible for the names, photographs, and other information about other people that Client publishes on the site, and confirms that Client has permission from each person named or pictured, and, for any person under eighteen (18), from that person’s parent or legal guardian. Studio may remove any information about a minor if Studio reasonably believes permission has not been given or the information could put the minor at risk.

2. PRICING, TERM, AND RENEWAL

2.1 Pricing.

  • 1-Year Plan: $50

  • 2-Year Plan: $80

  • 3-Year Plan: $110

2.2 Renewal. After the initial term, Client may renew for additional one-year periods at $50 per year. Renewal is not automatic unless Client affirmatively elects to renew.

2.3 Pre-Enrollment Disclosure. All pricing, term length, renewal rates, any applicable sales tax, and the domain release fee described in Section 3 will be clearly disclosed to Client before payment is collected.

2.4 Sales Tax. Studio charges Connecticut sales tax as required by Connecticut law. Clients located in Connecticut pay sales tax at the rate that applies to the use of the services (currently 1% for business use and 6.35% for personal use). Clients located outside Connecticut are not charged Connecticut sales tax. Any discount is applied before sales tax is calculated. A Client who enrolls for business use certifies that the site is for business use and that the business information Client provides is accurate.

3. DOMAIN RELEASE FEE

If Client wishes to transfer their domain to another registrar or hosting provider at the end of or during their term, a domain release fee of $25 applies. This fee covers the administrative cost of releasing the domain. This fee is disclosed at enrollment and is a condition of domain transfer. Client is not required to transfer their domain; the fee applies only if Client elects to do so.

4. PAYMENT TERMS

Payment for the selected Plan is due in full at enrollment. Studio does not issue invoices requesting payment; Studio provides a receipt for every payment. No work begins until payment is confirmed. The total amount due, including the selected Plan price and any applicable sales tax, will be displayed before payment is collected.

5.2 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS STATED IN SECTION 5.1, THE PROGRAM IS PROVIDED “AS IS” WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

5.3 No Results Warranty. Studio does not warrant any particular business outcome, search engine ranking, traffic volume, or revenue result from use of the Program.

9.1 Cancellation and Refunds. Client may cancel within seven (7) days of purchase and receive a full refund, except that if Studio has already registered Client’s domain, Studio keeps the domain registration cost. After seven (7) days, Client may cancel the Program at any time by written notice to Studio, but payments are not refundable and no pro-rated refund is owed for the unused portion of the term unless Studio is in material breach. Sales tax is refunded in the same proportion as the fees refunded.

9.2 Termination by Studio. Studio may terminate Client’s Program access if Client violates these terms, engages in abusive conduct, or uses the website for unlawful purposes. In such case, no refund is owed.

9.3 Studio Breach. If Studio fails to provide the Program as described and does not cure the failure within fifteen (15) days of written notice from Client, Client is entitled to a pro-rated refund for the unused portion of the term.

9.4 Domain Release on Termination. Upon termination for any reason, Client may elect to transfer their domain by paying the $25 domain release fee described in Section 3.

9.5 Survival. Sections 1.6, 6, 7, 8, 10, and 11 survive termination.

Any such court action shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts of New London County, Connecticut, and each party consents to personal jurisdiction in those courts.

10.6 Fees. Arbitration filing, administrative, and arbitrator fees are paid as provided in the applicable AAA rules. Each party pays its own attorney’s fees, unless the law or the arbitrator awards them.

10.7 Individual Claims Only. Claims may be brought only on an individual basis, not as a plaintiff or class member in any class, collective, or representative proceeding.

10.8 Waiver of Jury Trial. BY ACCEPTING THIS AGREEMENT, CLIENT AND STUDIO EACH GIVE UP THE RIGHT TO HAVE A DISPUTE DECIDED BY A JUDGE OR JURY, EXCEPT AS STATED IN SECTION 10.5.
