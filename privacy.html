Privacy Policy

How TEC Web Studio handles your information.

Last updated: [Insert date]

TEC Web Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use our Site Starter service or visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you use our services, including:

  • Name, email address, and contact details
  • Business name and details
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Website content you provide (text, photos, and related materials)
  • Any other information you choose to share with us in support requests or correspondence

We may also collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and device information when you visit our website or use our services. We collect this solely to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Site Starter service
  • Process payments and manage your account
  • Communicate with you about your project, support requests, renewals, or important notices
  • Review content before publication and enforce our Site Starter Agreement
  • Comply with legal obligations

Our team may access your account and content when needed to provide support or to enforce our terms.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate the business (for example, payment processors and hosting providers), under strict confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, or in response to valid legal requests from courts or law-enforcement agencies
  • When necessary to protect people from serious harm or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or sale of assets), in which case the new owner would be bound by this policy

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

After your service ends:

  • We keep your content for 90 days so you can renew or request an export
  • After that period we may delete it
  • We may retain certain records longer if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (for example, accounting or dispute resolution)

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time, subject to any records we are legally required to keep.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@tecwebstudio.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on our website and update the “Last updated” date. If a change is significant, we will notify you by email. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at:

info@tecwebstudio.com
